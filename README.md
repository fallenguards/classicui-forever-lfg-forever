# ClassicUI Forever: LFG Forever

LFG Forever's window in the classic look of [ClassicUI Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/classicui-forever), made to sit with its Looking For Group window. Install it when you use both addons.

- One window at a time: LFG Forever opens where the Looking For Group window stood, and the other closes.
- Its side tabs look and work like the Looking For Group window's: Create Listing and Group Browser take you back to those pages in the same spot.
- A classic double pane, as the professions window: your filters on the left, with Filters, Whispers and Player ratings tabs like the friends list's, and the groups and players found on the right.
- Classic checkboxes, drop downs, red buttons and scroll bars, in silver or in the Bronze Forever theme.

## How to use it

1. Have [ClassicUI Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/classicui-forever) with its Looking For Group window on, and [LFG Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/lfg-forever).
2. Install this addon. There is nothing to set up.
3. Open LFG Forever as you always do: its tab on the Looking For Group window, `/lfg`, or its minimap button.

With ClassicUI Forever's Looking For Group window turned off, this addon leaves LFG Forever as it is.
