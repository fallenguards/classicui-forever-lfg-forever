# Changelog

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-24

### Fixed
- LFG Forever's window can be dragged by its top again. It shares one spot with the Looking For Group window, so dragging either moves both (with ClassicUI Forever 0.65.0).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added
- LFG Forever's window in the ClassicUI Forever look, beside the Looking For Group window: the same spot and height, the Era eye in its corner, and only one of the two up at a time.
- A classic double pane, as the professions window: the filters on the left, laid out to fit, with Filters, Whispers and Player ratings tabs like the friends list's; the groups and players found on the right.
- Its side tabs wear ClassicUI Forever's. Create Listing and Group Browser open those pages in the same spot, and Who opens ClassicUI Forever's who list.
- Search and Role Check in the foot of the window, and a Close button, as the professions window has.
- Classic checkboxes, drop downs, red buttons and scroll bars, in silver or in the Bronze Forever theme.
